We're selective
for a reason.

FurCare isn't for everyone — and that's by design. We only work with sitters who genuinely love animals, have experience caring for pets, and understand the responsibility that comes with being trusted in someone's home.

Our vetting process is thorough because pet owners deserve peace of mind. If you're passionate about pet care and meet our standards, we'd love to have you.

Requirements

  • 18+ years old
  • Prior pet care experience (professional or personal)
  • Pass background check & reference verification
  • Complete a video or in-person interview
  • Live within FurCare's service area
  • Provide proof of pet care training or certifications (preferred)
